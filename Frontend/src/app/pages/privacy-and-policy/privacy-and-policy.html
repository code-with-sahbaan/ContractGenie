Privacy And Policy


Published and Effective: August 27, 2025

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how ContractGenie collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email, password (encrypted).
  • Uploaded Content: Contracts and documents you upload.
  • Usage Data: Chat interactions, queries, and logs.
  • Technical Data: Device, browser, and IP address.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide authentication and secure access.
  • To enable AI-powered features (contract retrieval, chatbot responses).
  • To improve the Service (analytics, troubleshooting).
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Use of AI Services

We use OpenAI’s API to process user prompts and generate responses.

  • Uploaded documents may be converted into vector embeddings for retrieval (stored in our database, not shared with third parties).
  • OpenAI may receive query content for processing but does not retain data beyond the processing request (see OpenAI’s Privacy Policy).

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Documents and embeddings are stored securely in PostgreSQL/pgvector.
  • All communication is secured via HTTPS.
  • JWT tokens protect user sessions.

5. Sharing of Data

  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • Data is only shared with third-party AI providers (e.g., OpenAI) to deliver chatbot functionality.

6. Your Rights

  • You may request deletion of your account and uploaded documents.
  • You may request a copy of your stored data.

7. Changes to Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page.
